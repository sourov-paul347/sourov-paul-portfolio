01 / About

Turning AI ideas into clear product problems.

As a beginner in AI product management, I’m focused on learning the full product loop: understanding users, framing problems, defining an MVP, collaborating with design and technology, and measuring whether a feature actually creates value.

🎯

My product mindset

Start with the problem—not the technology. Make assumptions visible, test them early, and use evidence to decide what to improve next.

“Useful AI should feel understandable, trustworthy, and purposeful.”

What I’m learning

AI product discovery, LLM workflows, product metrics, UX thinking, experimentation, responsible AI, and writing strong product requirements.
